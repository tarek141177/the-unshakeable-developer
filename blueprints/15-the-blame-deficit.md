### 📐 The Architectural Reality

Organizations are fundamentally social systems governed by chains of accountability. When core revenue platforms collapse, senior leadership demands an accountable owner who can explain why the disaster occurred and lead the recovery. No board of directors will ever accept 'the AI hallucinated' as an acceptable resolution.

Autonomous models cannot carry this burden because they have no career to damage, no professional standing to rebuild, and no moral capacity to bear consequence. This 'blame deficit' is not a temporary capability limit; it is a permanent structural law of organizational trust that guarantees software engineering remains anchored to humans.

> 💡 **Core Law:**  
> *"Trust flows through accountability chains, and accountability chains require someone who can lose something."*

---

### 🏃 The Monday Morning Move
Identify the most critical revenue system near you with ambiguous ownership. Formally message your manager proposing yourself as a primary or secondary system owner.
